About NEPSE GHAR

A practical app built to make Nepal market activity easier to follow and easier to use.

NEPSE GHAR is not just a market information app. It is a working tool for people who actively manage IPO applications, follow live market movement, review companies, track holdings, and want premium insights in a form that is easy to use on a phone.

The goal is simple: remove confusion, reduce repeated manual work, and give investors a clean place to do more of their important market tasks.
