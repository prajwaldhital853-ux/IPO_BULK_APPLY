About NEPSE GHAR

Focused on practical tools that reduce friction for everyday investors.

NEPSE GHAR is built around the workflows investors repeat most: checking active issues, managing IPO tasks, tracking holdings, watching key market signals, and unlocking premium analysis without juggling multiple disconnected sources.

Instead of acting like a generic fintech template, the platform is shaped specifically for Nepal's capital market behavior, data needs, and retail investor habits.
