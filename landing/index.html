About NEPSE GHAR

Nepali Share Market

NEPSE Ghar App is a modern mobile application designed especially for Nepali investors. It allows users to access and track the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) easily from the comfort of their home.

Our mission is to make Nepal share market accessible and visually engaging — from first-time investors to experienced traders.
