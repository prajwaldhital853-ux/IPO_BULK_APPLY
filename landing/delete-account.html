NEPSE GHAR

Delete your account

Last updated: 5 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your NEPSE GHAR account and associated data. NEPSE GHAR is the mobile app available at www.nepseghar.com.

1. Delete from the app (recommended)

  1. Open the NEPSE GHAR app.
  2. Sign in with the Google account you want to delete.
  3. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your app PIN when asked.
  5. Confirm deletion.

This removes your NEPSE GHAR server profile and local app data for that account on the device (including saved MeroShare accounts, PIN data, watchlist, portfolio cache, and related local storage).

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, email us from the same Google address used for your NEPSE GHAR account:

Email: kalash.financialsolutions@gmail.com

Include:

  • Subject: NEPSE GHAR account deletion request
  • Your Google account email
  • A short note that you want the account and associated data deleted

We aim to complete email deletion requests within 7 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Your NEPSE GHAR user profile on our servers
  • Subscription / premium status linked to that profile
  • Local app data for that account on devices where deletion is completed in-app

4. What may be kept

  • Limited records we may need for legal, fraud-prevention, or payment verification (for example payment screenshots or support emails), only as long as reasonably required
  • Data held by third parties you connected separately (Google, MeroShare / CDSC, banks) under their own policies — deleting NEPSE GHAR does not delete your Google or MeroShare accounts
